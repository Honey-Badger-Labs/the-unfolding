# Science policy

The Unfolding is allowed to be beautiful and strange. It is not allowed to mislead.

## Labels

| Label | Use it when | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Observed | Telescopes have seen this kind of thing directly | A ring of light around a black hole |
| Model-dependent | It follows from observations through a model with assumptions | The first 380,000 years |
| Artistic interpretation | It is a visual metaphor or an invented bridge | Reading an eye in a nebula |

A stage can show a procedural shape and still be **Observed** if the note says the shape is procedural.

## Rules

- Every event cites at least one source in `data/sources.json`, and every source says which claim it supports.
- Dates are ranges. Future events are marked `future: true` and say "from now".
- Exaggerations are stated: "sizes are exaggerated", "the order here is a creative choice".
- The Big Bang is space expanding everywhere, never an explosion into empty space.
- The story is about matter organising itself into complexity. It never claims the universe was aiming at us.

## Known simplifications

- Planet sizes and orbital speeds are scaled for visibility.
- The cosmic web, galaxies and nebulae are procedural, not survey data or images.
- The journey follows one line of descent (web → galaxy → star → planets) and skips the many generations of stars that made the heavy elements.
